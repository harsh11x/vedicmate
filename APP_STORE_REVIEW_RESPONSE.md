# App Store Review Reply

Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for the feedback. We have updated the app experience and clarified the business model.

## Guideline 4.3(b) - Design / Spam

Vedic Mate is not intended to be another generic horoscope or fortune-telling app. In this build, we updated the primary app experience to emphasize the broader product:

- Live pooja participation and ritual booking
- Spiritual education through scripture/library content
- Yoga and wellness routines
- Daily habit tracking and reflection journaling
- Cultural history timeline content
- Physical remedy product shopping and checkout
- Live guidance chat/call sessions for reflective and cultural wellness support

We also reduced prediction-focused wording in the main UI. The home screen no longer leads with generic astrology categories such as Palm Reading, Numerology, or Vedic Astrology. Guide listings now use wellness/guidance language and include a clear disclaimer that content is reflective, educational, and cultural, not guaranteed future prediction.

## Guideline 2.1(b) - Information Needed

1. Who are the users that will use the paid subscriptions, features, and services in the app?

Users are individuals who want access to cultural wellness tools, live spiritual guidance sessions, live pooja booking, remedy products, journaling, habit tracking, and educational content. There is no active auto-renewable subscription sold in this build.

2. Where can users purchase the subscriptions, features, and services that can be accessed in the app?

In this build, users can purchase wallet recharge credits and physical remedy products inside the app through Razorpay checkout. Wallet credits are used for eligible live guidance chat/call sessions inside Vedic Mate. Physical products are purchased through the Remedies cart and Checkout flow. No subscription purchase is currently active.

3. What specific types of previously purchased subscriptions, features, and services can a user access in the app?

Users can access their in-app wallet balance, live guidance sessions paid with wallet credits, order history for physical remedy product purchases, and saved app content such as profile, journal, and wellness activity data. There are no previously purchased subscriptions enabled in this build.

4. What paid content, subscriptions, or features are unlocked within the app that do not use In-App Purchase?

Wallet recharge credits are used for live guidance services. Physical goods such as remedy products are purchased through the app checkout. These are service/physical-commerce flows handled through Razorpay. Auto-renewable subscriptions and digital subscription unlocks are not active in this build.

5. Can users purchase physical goods or services together with digital content in your app?

Physical remedy products are purchased through the Remedies checkout flow. Live guidance services are paid separately through wallet credits. The app does not bundle a physical product purchase with a digital subscription in a single purchase. If a product or service includes related informational instructions, those instructions support the physical/service purchase and are not sold as a separate digital subscription.

We hope this clarifies the current app behavior and business model.
